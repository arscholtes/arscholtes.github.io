The design argument

02

Most multi-session setups are task-parallel: N sessions on N tickets. That reliably hits the review queue as its ceiling, because generation parallelises and human review does not.

Role separation is cheaper because the sessions produce different output types. Only forge generates diff. Pilot produces answers; shepherd produces merges. They do not compete for the same scarce resource.

The cost is different too. The risk is not throughput, it is drift — whichever session happens to be focused absorbing whatever you just thought of. The charters exist for that.

The “never” list is not documentation after the fact. It is the shape of the system.
